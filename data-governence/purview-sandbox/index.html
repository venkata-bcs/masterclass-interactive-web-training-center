Data Governance & Microsoft Purview Enterprise IT Architecture Zero to Hero Curriculum

Enterprise Data Governance, Cataloging & Regulatory Lineage

Establishing the foundational guardrails of trust, compliance, security, and classification across multi-cloud databases, lakehouses, and AI training pipelines.

Lead Instructor Architectural Insight (© Venkata Bhattaram): "Without rigorous Data Governance, a Data Lake inevitably degrades into a toxic Data Swamp. When sensitive customer data is leaked, regulations like GDPR or HIPAA are violated with millions in fines, and AI models hallucinate on unverified records. Governance is not bureaucracy—it is the digital chain of custody that makes data valuable, secure, and legally actionable."
Pillar 01 • Catalyst

Why It Exists

Unregulated enterprise data estates face severe regulatory fines, unauthorized data exposure, orphan tables, and AI compliance failures. Organizations must know where their sensitive data lives, who owns it, and how it flows.

  • Prevents multimillion-dollar regulatory fines under GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and EU AI Act
  • Eliminates dark data and shadow IT across fragmented cloud subscriptions
  • Establishes automated end-to-end lineage from source OLTP systems to executive dashboards
  • Ensures AI models are trained exclusively on audited, consent-verified datasets
Pillar 02 • Foundations

What It Is

The holistic framework of policies, metadata discovery, automated classification, end-to-end lineage tracking, and data stewardship enforced through unified platforms like Microsoft Purview.

  • Data Map & Discovery: Automated crawlers indexing metadata across cloud and on-prem
  • Classification & Sensitivity Labels: Regex and NLP tagging for SSNs, PII, and financial data
  • Column-Level Lineage: Visual graphs tracing data origins and downstream impact
  • Business Glossary & Stewardship: Formal data asset ownership and dictionary definitions
Pillar 03 • Mechanics

How It Works

Purview automated scanning engines crawl storage accounts and relational databases. Classifiers inspect data samples, sensitivity labels are applied automatically, and lineage telemetry is extracted from SQL query plans and Spark execution logs.

  • Automated Scanning: Connectors for Azure SQL, ADLS Gen2, Snowflake, AWS S3, and Power BI
  • Lineage Extraction: Apache Atlas REST APIs and OpenLineage event streaming
  • Access & Security Policies: Integration with Microsoft Entra ID and RBAC policies
  • Data Stewardship Workflows: Automated review approval loops for glossary terms
Pillar 04 • Production

Where It Is Used

Required by law and enterprise policy across banking, healthcare, retail, pharmaceuticals, and public sector organizations.

  • Global Banking & Capital Markets: BCBS 239 risk data aggregation and SOX compliance
  • Healthcare Systems & Insurers: Protecting PHI / HIPAA records across analytics platforms
  • Mergers & Acquisitions: Auditing newly acquired enterprise data estates for technical debt
  • Enterprise AI Governance: Verifying data provenance and licensing rights for LLM corpora
