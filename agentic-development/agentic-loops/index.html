The agentic loop.
Observe. Think. Act. Check. Repeat.

Every agent runs the same loop: it observes where things stand, thinks about the next step, acts with a tool, then checks the result. It goes round until a stop rule fires. Pick a mission below to see three common loop patterns, and one loop that would run forever without its stop rules.

    What you just saw

    Three loop patterns, and why stop rules matter

    REACT

    Reason and act

    Think one step, act, look at the result, think again. Simple and flexible, good when the path is unknown.

    PLAN & EXECUTE

    Plan first, re-plan on failure

    Write the whole plan up front, then work through it. Cheaper and easier to follow, and it re-plans when the numbers say it is off track.

    REFLECTION

    Draft, critique, revise

    A second agent, the Critic, reviews each draft against clear checks. Quality goes up with every pass, until the checks pass.

    STOP RULES

    Know when to quit

    Without them, a loop retries a broken service all night. With them, it stops, keeps the trace, and hands over to a person.
